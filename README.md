# 🌌 ChronoCosmos | Celestial Age & Zodiac Explorer

> A flashy, cosmic web application that calculates your precise chronological age in real-time down to the second, greets you according to the current time (AM/PM), and reveals your complete astrological zodiac identity! ✨🚀

---

## 🌟 Features

- ⏳ **Live-Ticking Age Engine**: Precision counter calculating years, months, days, hours, minutes, and seconds that ticks in real-time.
- ☀️ **Time-Aware AM/PM Greetings**: Dynamically identifies morning, afternoon, evening, and night with personalized greetings and live 12-hour clock.
- ♈ **Complete Astrological Profile**: 
  - Dynamic zodiac sign calculation with glyphs (♈, ♉, ♊, etc.)
  - Elemental glowing card themes (Fire 🔥, Earth 🌍, Air 💨, Water 🌊)
  - Ruling planets, modality, celestial stone, and personality traits.
- 🎂 **Next Birthday Countdown**: Live progress bar counting down to your next planetary solar return.
- ⚡ **Cosmic Life Metrics**: Estimates of total solar days lived, heartbeats sustained, breaths drawn, and millions of kilometers traveled through space.
- 🌠 **Flashy Aesthetics**: Interactive background starfield canvas, glowing cosmic glassmorphism cards, and celebratory stardust confetti bursts.
